What the app is

SCOTUS Books (“the app”) is a personal program that turns published opinions of the Supreme Court of the United States into audio readings and uploads them to its owner's YouTube channel. It is used only by its owner.

YouTube Terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the app's handling of data is described in its Privacy Policy and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

The readings

The opinions read in the videos are works of the United States government and are in the public domain. The readings leave out footnotes and legal citations and are produced by a synthetic voice, so they may contain mistakes in wording or pronunciation. They are not legal advice and are not an official record. The official text of each opinion, linked from every video, is the authoritative version.
