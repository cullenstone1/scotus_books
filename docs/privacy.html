SCOTUS Books

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

SCOTUS Books (“the app”) is a personal program that uploads audio readings of Supreme Court opinions to its owner's YouTube channel. It is operated by one person, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

The app asks the owner to sign in with Google and grant permission to manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to upload new videos to that channel, to set their titles, descriptions, chapters and thumbnails, to create playlists on that channel and add its own uploads to them, to list that channel's own playlists so it doesn't create duplicates, and to read back the upload status and public or private visibility of the videos it has uploaded, which it shows on this website's list of published readings. It does not read, collect or use any other information from the account, and it does not access any other person's account or data.

What the app stores

The app stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google in a file on the owner's own computer, so that uploads can run without signing in each time. It also keeps a local record of which videos it has uploaded and their YouTube video and playlist IDs. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the app, and the app collects no information about viewers of the videos.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer any user data to third parties. The only data it sends anywhere is the videos and their descriptions, which go to YouTube.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind. This website does not either.

Revoking access

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's stored access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
