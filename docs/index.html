Supreme Court of the United States

SCOTUS Books

Opinions of the Supreme Court of the United States, read aloud in full and published as videos on YouTube.

What it is

Each reading covers the syllabus, the opinion of the Court, and every concurring and dissenting opinion, with a chapter for each. Footnotes and legal citations are left out so the text can be followed by ear. Nothing is summarized or added, and there is no commentary.

The readings use a synthetic text-to-speech voice. Each video links to the official text of the opinion on supremecourt.gov, which includes the footnotes and citations.

How it works

A small program checks the Court's website for newly released opinions, extracts the text from the official PDF, removes footnotes and citations, records the reading, and uploads it to the project's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The source code is on GitHub.

Every reading published so far is listed on the opinions page, with links to the video and the official text.
